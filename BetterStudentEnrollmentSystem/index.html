Welcome to the Online Enrollment System

The MAIT Online Enrollment System is a web-based platform for seamless student registration at Maharaja Agrasen Institute of Technology (MAIT), Delhi.
It allows students to enroll in B.Tech programs (CSE, IT, ECE, EEE, MAE) efficiently.
Key features include course selection, secure registration, a dynamic UI, and real-time updates, making the admission process faster, transparent, and convenient.
